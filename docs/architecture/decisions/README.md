## Template

Copy this into `NNNN-short-title.md` with the next free number.

```markdown
# NNNN Title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected
Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Deciders: TBD

## Context
What forces the decision. Facts only, with their source.

## Decision
What is decided, in one or two paragraphs.

## Alternatives
Each option considered and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must now be maintained.
```
